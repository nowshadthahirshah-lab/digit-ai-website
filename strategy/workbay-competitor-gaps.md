# Workbay vs Flowly vs Garage Shift – gap list (25/11/2026)

## Garage Shift (garageshift.co.uk) – what it has
- Price: £1 first month, then £50/month or £500/year. No minimum term.
- Live demo, no account/card/call (Workbay already has "Try the demo").
- Customer ONLINE BOOKING page (branded, live availability from working hours, garage approves requests).
- "Smart Area Match" for mobile mechanics: suggests days you're already nearby.
- DVSA lookup fills make, model, MOT and tax; MOT dates checked DAILY against DVSA; one reminder per MOT with a link to book back in.
- Quotes that convert into jobsheets in one click; quote win-rate on dashboard.
- Price book / parts catalogue with margins.
- Health checks with photos + TYRE TREAD depth report (NSF/OSF/NSR/OSR mm).
- Invoices by email + WhatsApp, automatic VAT; LIVE XERO SYNC (retry failed syncs).
- Dashboard "To do": confirm booking requests, book in accepted quotes, invoice finished jobs; money: invoiced / paid / awaiting; where revenue came from (booked/quoted/quick).
- Separate pages: Garages vs Mobile mechanics; 14 feature pages; Tutorials (videos), Blog, Roadmap, FAQs.
- Trust: customer logo wall, 3 named reviews, support <5 hrs, free setup + branding + FREE DATA MIGRATION.
