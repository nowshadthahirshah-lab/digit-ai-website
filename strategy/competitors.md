## What this means for Workbay
1. **Price:** Workbay (£29) is joint-cheapest with Workauto (£30) and 2–5× cheaper than most. Lead with it: "Garage software from £29. Others charge £65–£150."
2. **Trial gap:** rivals give 14–30 days. 7 days is the shortest – consider 14 days, or keep 7 but push "no card".
3. **Feature gaps vs rivals (worth checking/roadmap):** DVLA/DVSA reg lookup with MOT dates, automatic SMS reminders (Workbay = tap to send), online booking, Xero/QuickBooks/MTD export, multi-user.
4. **Workbay's edge to market:** phone-first, WhatsApp invoices, profit rings, set up in 2 minutes, no contract, no setup fee.
5. **Content angle:** "Paying £100+ a month for garage software?" comparison Reels and carousels (no competitor logos; state facts with dates).
6. **Workauto is the one to watch** – same price, same audience. Check their Instagram/TikTok weekly.
