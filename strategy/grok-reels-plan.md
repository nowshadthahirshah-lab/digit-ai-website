# Workbay – "Pick one" Reels plan (copied from @scratchaiapp format)

## Daily routine (15 min)
1. Open Grok → Imagine → paste today's VIDEO PROMPT → make 6–10s 9:16 video.
2. Download it. Open Instagram → Reel → add it.
3. Add on-screen TEXT (Instagram "Aa"): question at top, two words big in the middle.
4. Add trending audio (no voice needed).
5. Paste CAPTION. Post. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
