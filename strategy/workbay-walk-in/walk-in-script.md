# Workbay walk-in guide

**Goal:** visit 10 independent garages and get 2 to say "yes, I want it".
When 2 say yes: register with the ICO, go live, then set those garages up yourself.

**Take with you:**
- Your phone with the demo open: https://claude.ai/artifact/M1bCU3Gjr5uVQ9K6bkjpUg (it opens straight on the demo dashboard). Check it loads before you walk in.
- Printed leaflets (`workbay-leaflet.pdf`). Write your mobile number on the line at the bottom before you go.
- This page, or the tracker at the bottom on your phone.

---

## When to go

- **Best:** about 10:00 to 11:30 and 14:00 to 16:00, when cars are on the ramps and the owner isn't rushing.
- **Avoid:** first thing in the morning, lunchtime, Monday mornings and Saturdays.
- Ask for the **owner or manager**. If they're busy under a car, ask when to come back, leave a leaflet and write down the time.

---

## The 2-minute visit

### 1. Say hello (10 seconds)
> "Hi, I'm Bilal. I'm local, from Walthamstow. I've built a simple app for independent garages. Can I show you something on my phone? Two minutes, and I'll leave you alone after."

### 2. Ask one question (20 seconds)
Pick one, then listen. Their answer tells you what to show.
> "How do you keep track of jobs and invoices at the moment? Paper, WhatsApp, a spreadsheet?"

> "Do you ever have customers who take ages to pay?"

> "How do you remind people when their MOT is due?"

### 3. Show 3 things only (60 seconds)
Hold the phone so they can see it. Tap, don't explain too much.

1. **Dashboard:** "This is your morning. Who owes you money, who's overdue, which MOTs are due. Tap the green button and the WhatsApp message is already written."
2. **A job → Health check → Extra work to approve:** "You mark brakes Urgent and put £120. The customer gets a link, taps Yes on their phone, and it goes straight onto the invoice."
3. **The invoice:** "Itemised, with your bank details. Send it on WhatsApp in one tap. You can take a deposit too."

If they mentioned MOTs, show **More → MOT and service reminders** instead of number 3.

### 4. Price, honestly (15 seconds)
> "It's 7 days free, then £29 a month, or £290 a year. A card is needed to start, and you can cancel any time. It works on any phone, with no app store."

### 5. The ask (15 seconds)
> "I'm starting with a few local garages first, and I'll set it up with you myself: your details, your logo, your first customers. Can I put you down? I'll call you when it's ready."

If yes, write down their **name, garage, mobile, and the best day and time to call**. Give them a leaflet.
If no, say thank you, leave a leaflet, and ask: "What would make it useful for you?" Write their answer down. It's valuable.

## When they say...

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I use paper / WhatsApp, it works fine." | "It does. Workbay keeps the WhatsApp part, it just writes the messages for you and remembers who owes you." |
| "I don't have time." | "That's why I'll set it up with you. It takes about 10 minutes, and it saves time every invoice after that." |
| "£29 is a lot." | "If it chases one late invoice or books one extra MOT a month, it's paid for itself. And the first 7 days are free." |
| "I already use another system." | "No problem. What do you like about it, and what annoys you?" Write it down. |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Each garage can only see its own records, card payments go through Stripe, and it's all explained on the Privacy page." |
| "Can I try it first?" | "Yes, 7 days free. I'll set it up with you so you're not on your own." |

**Only promise what Workbay does today.** Number plate look-up is "coming soon". Don't promise SMS texts or Xero sync, because those aren't built yet.

## After the visit

- **Same day:** send a short WhatsApp: "Hi [name], it's Bilal from Workbay. Thanks for your time today. I'll message you as soon as it's ready to set up."
- Mark them hot, warm or cold in the tracker.
- When you have **2 hot garages**: tell me and Ali. We register with the ICO, go live, and you book a time to set each one up.
